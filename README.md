Geotone provides a live, data‑driven wire of the world’s most significant news stories, ranking them by volume, tone, and geographic reach. Leveraging the GDELT Project, it turns millions of articles into real‑time geopolitical risk signals for analysts, AI agents, and businesses. The platform offers dashboards, timelines, alerts, and detailed analytics on topics, countries, and entities, helping users understand global media coverage and emerging events.

**Areas:** Company · News · Media-Analytics · Geopolitics · Data

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Geotone: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
